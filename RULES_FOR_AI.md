# Rules for AI (strict)

- Follow `START_HERE.md` + `README.md` + `SCOPE.md` + `DEMO.md` + `STATUS.md` exactly.
- Do NOT add anything listed as out of scope.
- Work in small steps: **max 2–3 files per step**.
- After every step:
  1) write a short changelog
  2) write the exact run/test steps
  3) ensure the app still runs
- Keep the weekly flow simple and stable:
  - voting: allow voting, show results
  - discussion: allow comments, show comments
  - closed: read-only
- Demo reliability > extra features. No large refactors.
